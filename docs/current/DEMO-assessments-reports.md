## 3. Rules the system now enforces (server and database)

- **Publication state** is the latest event per assessment: none = not published, `publish` = published, `unpublish` = unpublished. Events are immutable, carry actor, reason, expected and recorded pupil counts and whether a gap was confirmed, and are keyed by a client UUID: the same actor and body replay the original event (200), any other use is `request_reused` (409).
- **Publish needs**: a whole-number maximum, an exact calendar day, no invalid or duplicate results or enrolments, at least one recorded result, and explicit confirmation when some current pupils have no result. A missing row stays "no result"; it is never turned into a zero.
- **Unpublish needs** a reason of 10 to 500 characters and is refused while a published report that has not been replaced uses the results.
- **Locks**: while published, assessment details cannot change (409 `published`) and results change only through the reviewed correction path that already records actor, reason and before/after.
- **Edit details** is compare-and-set on title, type, maximum, date and updated time; a reason is required once any result is saved; a maximum below a saved score is refused; every edit stores before and after snapshots.
- **Reports**: only published, valid, single-pair results can be compiled; lines are frozen snapshots (title, type, day, score, maximum, school grade); private feedback is never copied. Publishing a report whose lines no longer match the published results is refused (`results_changed`) and sends no notice. The staff view marks lines changed since compile; family and learner views never receive that flag.
- **Correction**: a replacement draft names the published report it corrects and a reason. Once the replacement is published the original stays readable and shows "replaced by"; families see both.
- **Privacy**: guardians and learners read only published reports of their linked learner; unlinked accounts and unassigned staff get 404/403; unpublished results and drafts are never in a family response.

## 7. Limits and what is incomplete

- No grade rubric, family-visible raw scores, bulk import or assessment deletion exist; those need policy owners.
- A report's overall grade and summary are typed by the school; nothing is calculated.
- `POST /api/assessments` and `POST /api/report-cards` have no client replay key, so an unconfirmed save asks the person to check the list; publish, unpublish and detail edit are replay-safe and the pages retry with the same request id.
- The real hosted D1 runner was not available; portability is shown by native SQLite prepares under the hosted limits, not by the hosted service.
- Manual UAT cases need named educators and administrators.
- Data-dictionary header counts (41 tables, 338 columns, migrations 0000-0016) were left as written; migration 0020 adds four tables and their sections are new.

It had no connections and no parent; it was terminated. Mounted wrappers should trap SIGTERM and always stop the worker.
